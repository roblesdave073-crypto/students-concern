ABOUT THE SYSTEM

A clear path from concern to solution

The Student Complaint and Concerns Management System gives students an organized way to report problems, and gives administrators and staff the tools to review, assign, respond to, and resolve them, with a complete record of every action.

StudentsSubmit and track concerns
Faculty and staffProcess and resolve cases
AdministratorsReview, assign, and report
SYSTEM FEATURES

Everything a concern needs

Easy submission

Choose a category and department, describe the concern, and attach photos or documents.

Unique tracking number

Every complaint gets its own number so you can follow its status and history.

Instant notifications

Get alerts when your complaint is reviewed, assigned, answered, or resolved.

Anonymous option

Report sensitive concerns without your identity shown to unauthorized viewers.

Staff assignment

Administrators route each case to the right staff member for action.

Reports and analytics

Charts by category and department, printable reports, and CSV export.
